# Autonomous Finance Reference Architecture

## Purpose

Provide a common architecture for designing and transforming finance enterprises across the 12 Finance Architecture Streams.

## Layered Architecture

### 1. Business Layer
- Enterprise strategy
- Finance strategy
- Finance operating model
- Value streams
- Capabilities
- Policies
- Controls
- KPIs

### 2. Process Layer
- R2R
- P2P
- O2C
- Tax
- Treasury
- FP&A
- Controlling
- Asset Accounting
- GRC
- Finance Analytics
- AI Finance
- Connected Finance

### 3. Data Layer
- Chart of accounts
- Legal entities
- Cost/profit centers
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Banks
- Assets
- Tax attributes
- Planning dimensions
- Risk/control data
- Transactional and analytical data

### 4. Application Layer
- ERP
- AP/AR
- Procurement
- Billing
- Tax engines
- Treasury
- Planning

- GRC
- Analytics
- AI services
- Integration platforms

### 5. Technology Layer

- APIs
- Events
- Integration

- Observability
- Automation
- Data platforms

### 6. Intelligence Layer
- Analytics
- Forecasting
- Machine learning
- GenAI
- AI agents

- Autonomous workflows

### 7. Control & Governance Layer
- Internal controls
- SoD
- Audit
- Regulatory compliance
- Data privacy
- Model governance
- Policy enforcement

## Architecture Questions

Every finance solution should answer:

1. What business capability is improved?
2. What process is changed?
3. What data is authoritative?
4. What controls are required?
5. What applications enable the process?
6. What integration contracts exist?
7. What security and SoD constraints apply?
8. What automation is possible?
9. Where should AI be introduced?
10. What measurable business outcome proves value?
