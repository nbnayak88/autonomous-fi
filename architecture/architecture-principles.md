# Finance Architecture Principles

1. **Business Centricity & Finance Agility** — architecture starts with enterprise value.
2. **Finance Data is the New Core** — financial data has explicit ownership, quality, lineage and semantic governance.
3. **Open & Connected Financial Ecosystem** — finance operates across ERP, banks, tax, planning, procurement, risk and external partners.
4. **API-Led Integration** — connect capabilities through governed APIs and events.
5. **Experience-Led Finance** — design for finance professionals, business users and external partners.
6. **Scalable by Design** — support global entities, volumes, currencies, charts, regulations and scenarios.
7. **Security, Privacy & SoD by Design** — embed identity, authorization and segregation of duties into every finance solution.
8. **Automate First!** — remove repetitive manual work before adding complexity.
9. **AI with Human Accountability** — AI recommendations and agents operate within financial controls and approved authority.
10. **Continuous Close & Continuous Insight** — reduce latency from transaction to decision.
11. **Control by Design** — controls are embedded in process and data flows.
12. **Value, Cash, Risk & Compliance as Outcomes** — measure what finance enables for the enterprise.
