## 12 Architecture Streams

Enterprise Architect • Business Architect • Integration Architect • Domain Architect • Cloud & Infrastructure Architect • Application & Process Architect • AI Architect • Security Architect • Industry Architect • Data Architect • UI/UX Architect • Technology Architect

## Sector Architecture Principle

The finance capability remains common; **the architecture context changes by sector**. For example, Energy emphasizes asset intensity and transition economics, Financials emphasizes regulatory and risk controls, Health Care emphasizes care economics and compliance, and Information Technology emphasizes digital-product and ecosystem economics.

## Completion Model

**132 Capstones = 12 Finance Streams × 11 GICS Sectors.**

Each learner can select a sector and progressively build a complete Finance Architecture portfolio across all 12 streams.
