# Signature Connected Finance Framework

## 1. CONNECT
Connect Finance processes, systems, partners and authorities.

**Question:** What must exchange information?

## 2. CONTRACT
Define APIs, events, data contracts, ownership and controls.

**Question:** What exactly is exchanged, by whom and under what rules?

## 3. CONTROL
Secure identities, access, financial controls, compliance and auditability.

**Question:** Can we trust the connection?

## 4. CONTEXT
Create trusted Finance data and business context.

**Question:** Can Finance understand what is happening?

## 5. CONVERT
Convert connected data into analytics, decisions and workflow actions.

**Question:** What decision or action should the connection enable?

## 6. AUTOMATE
Automate repeatable Finance work and introduce governed AI agents.

**Question:** What can safely happen without manual intervention?

## 7. EVOLVE
Measure, learn, optimize and continuously improve the connected enterprise.

**Question:** How does Finance become better every cycle?

**Signature:**  
**CONNECT → CONTRACT → CONTROL → CONTEXT → CONVERT → AUTOMATE → EVOLVE**

# Architecture Challenge

## Challenge: Design a Connected Autonomous Finance Enterprise

### Business Scenario

A global enterprise runs SAP S/4HANA Finance across multiple countries. Finance is connected to banks, tax authorities, suppliers, customers, business networks, planning platforms and analytics solutions.

Current problems include:

- Point-to-point integrations
- Delayed financial information
- Reconciliation effort
- Payment exceptions
- Invoice exceptions
- Cash-application delays
- Regulatory integration complexity
- Data-quality defects
- Fragmented monitoring
- Manual close activities
- Limited forecasting agility
- Increasing demand for AI and autonomous Finance

### Your Mission

Design the target **Connected Finance architecture** that evolves the enterprise from fragmented integration to governed autonomous Finance.
