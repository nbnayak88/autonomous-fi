# Applied SAP Finance — AGR9 Governance, Risk & Compliance

> **Finance Architecture Stream: AGR9 | Governance, Risk & Compliance | Financial Governance & Risk**

## North Star

Design governance, risk, and compliance as a **connected control system** where obligations become measurable risks, risks become accountable controls, controls generate trusted evidence, and exceptions drive timely remediation.

## Master Value Stream

`Obligation → Risk → Control → Execution → Evidence → Exception → Remediation → Validation → Reporting → Assurance`

## Core Architecture Lens

GRC connects:

- **Business:** governance, policies, risk appetite, accountability, operating processes
- **Finance:** financial controls, close controls, payment controls, reporting integrity
- **Risk:** enterprise, operational, financial, technology, third-party, compliance risks
- **Security:** identity, access, segregation of duties, privileged access
- **Data:** risk/control/evidence data, regulatory obligations, audit trails
- **Technology:** SAP GRC capabilities, S/4HANA, IAM, workflow, analytics, security platforms
- **AI:** continuous monitoring, evidence automation, anomaly detection, regulatory intelligence, risk recommendations

## Architecture Outcomes

By the end of the journey, the learner should be able to:
1. Explain the relationship between governance, risk, controls, compliance, audit, and assurance.
2. Build risk taxonomies, control libraries, ownership models, and compliance mappings.
3. Architect integrated GRC across SAP business processes, security, IAM, audit, data, and analytics.
4. Design SoD, access governance, continuous monitoring, evidence, issue remediation, and reporting.
5. Diagnose control failures and distinguish process, configuration, access, data, and governance causes.
6. Identify AI opportunities for continuous assurance and risk intelligence.
7. Produce a target-state Digital GRC architecture and transformation roadmap.

## Core SAP Ecosystem

Typical solution components may include **SAP GRC capabilities, SAP S/4HANA, SAP Access Control, SAP Process Control, SAP Risk Management, SAP Audit Management, identity and access platforms, SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP Datasphere, SAP Integration Suite, and SAP Business AI/Joule**, depending on the enterprise landscape.
