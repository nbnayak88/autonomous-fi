# Applied SAP Finance — AFI0 Finance Analytics & Intelligence

> **Finance Architecture Stream: AFI0 | Finance Analytics & Intelligence | Financial Intelligence**

## North Star

Transform finance data into **trusted, explainable, timely intelligence** so finance leaders can see what happened, understand why, anticipate what may happen, and act on the right decision.

## Master Value Stream

`Source Data → Semantic Model → Metric → Analysis → Insight → Decision → Action → Outcome`

## Core Architecture Lens

Finance Intelligence connects:

- **Business:** CFO questions, performance, profitability, cash, working capital, risk, planning
- **Finance:** GL, AP, AR, Treasury, FP&A, Controlling, Assets, Tax
- **Operations:** sales, procurement, supply chain, projects, workforce, asset drivers
- **Data:** transactional data, master data, operational drivers, semantic models, external data
- **Technology:** SAP S/4HANA, SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP Datasphere, Integration Suite, enterprise data platforms
- **AI:** predictive analytics, natural-language insight, anomaly detection, recommendations, finance agents

## Architecture Outcomes

By the end of the journey, the learner should be able to:
1. Translate CFO and business questions into measurable analytics requirements.
2. Design governed financial KPIs, semantic models, data products, and lineage.
3. Architect Finance Analytics across S/4HANA, Datasphere, SAC, and connected enterprise data.
4. Design executive dashboards and decision experiences rather than report inventories.
5. Diagnose metric, data-quality, reconciliation, integration, security, and performance issues.
6. Identify predictive and generative AI opportunities for finance intelligence.
7. Produce a target-state Finance Intelligence architecture and transformation roadmap.

## Core SAP Ecosystem

Typical solution components may include **SAP S/4HANA Finance, SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP Datasphere, SAP Integration Suite, SAP Business AI/Joule, SAP Business Technology Platform, and connected enterprise data sources**.
