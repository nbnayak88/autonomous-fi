# Applied SAP Finance — ATR5 Treasury & Risk Management

> **Finance Architecture Stream: ATR5 | Treasury & Risk | Autonomous Treasury Operations**

## North Star

Design Treasury so the enterprise has **real-time visibility of cash, resilient liquidity, controlled payments, optimized funding and investments, measurable financial risk, connected banking, and increasingly autonomous decisions**.

## Master Value Stream

`Cash Forecast → Liquidity Position → Exposure/Risk → Funding/Investment Decision → Payment/Collection → Bank Execution → Statement → Reconciliation → Accounting → Risk & Treasury Insight`

## Core Architecture Lens

Treasury connects:

- **Business:** cash inflows/outflows, funding needs, investments, commercial exposures
- **Finance:** accounting, cash, liquidity, payments, close
- **Banking:** accounts, statements, payment channels, confirmations
- **Risk:** FX, interest rate, liquidity, counterparty, market risk
- **Data:** transactions, positions, forecasts, market data, bank data
- **AI:** predictive cash forecasting, anomaly detection, payment intelligence, autonomous recommendations

## Architecture Outcomes

By the end of the journey, the learner should be able to:
1. Explain enterprise Treasury, cash management, liquidity, banking, and financial risk.
2. Architect cash, payments, bank connectivity, funding, investment, exposure, and risk capabilities together.
3. Design controls for payments, bank accounts, liquidity, deals, valuation, and reconciliation.
4. Diagnose Treasury failures using transaction, market-data, master-data, integration, bank, and accounting evidence.
5. Design automation and AI opportunities for Treasury operations.
6. Define KPIs such as forecast accuracy, liquidity coverage, cash visibility, payment straight-through processing, bank reconciliation rate, exposure, and funding cost.
7. Produce a target-state Autonomous Treasury architecture and transformation roadmap.

## Core SAP Ecosystem

Typical solution components may include **SAP S/4HANA, SAP Treasury and Risk Management, SAP Cash Management, SAP Fiori, SAP Integration Suite, bank connectivity, payment platforms, market-data providers, SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP Business AI/Joule, and enterprise data platforms**.
