# Applied SAP Finance — ATX4 Tax Management & Compliance

> **Finance Architecture Stream: ATX4 | Tax Management | Digital Tax Enterprises**

## North Star

Design tax operations so that enterprise transactions are **tax-correct, compliant, traceable, reportable, electronically connected, audit-ready, and increasingly automated**.

## Master Value Stream

`Business Transaction → Tax Determination → Calculation → Accounting → Document/E-Invoice → Statutory Reporting → Filing/Submission → Reconciliation → Audit Evidence → Regulatory Change`

## Core Architecture Lens

Tax is not only a Finance activity. It connects:

- **Business:** sales, procurement, logistics, services, assets
- **Finance:** accounting, AP, AR, close, reporting
- **Compliance:** tax rules, filings, controls, audit
- **Technology:** SAP S/4HANA, SAP DRC, tax engines, authorities, integration
- **Data:** tax codes, rates, jurisdictions, registrations, customer/supplier data
- **AI:** regulatory intelligence, exception detection, document validation, automation

## Architecture Outcomes

By the end of the journey, the learner should be able to:
1. Explain the enterprise tax lifecycle and why tax accuracy begins with business transactions.
2. Understand indirect/direct tax, GST/VAT concepts, e-invoicing, statutory reporting, and localization at a conceptual level.
3. Architect SAP S/4HANA and SAP DRC within a connected digital tax ecosystem.
4. Design controls for tax determination, reporting, submission, reconciliation, and audit evidence.
5. Diagnose tax and compliance failures using transaction, master-data, configuration, integration, and authority evidence.
6. Design automation and AI opportunities for digital tax operations.
7. Produce a target-state Digital Tax Enterprise architecture and rollout roadmap.

## Core SAP Ecosystem

Typical solution components may include **SAP S/4HANA, SAP Document and Reporting Compliance, SAP Fiori, SAP Integration Suite, tax engines/services, government portals, e-invoicing networks, SAP Analytics Cloud, workflow/automation, and enterprise data platforms**.
